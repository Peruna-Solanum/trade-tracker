About:

This is Trade Tracker. You can input trade details, pics, etc. and will be able to automatically find the stats on your data without a subscription or doing things by hand. You can also factor in custom strategies and compare how well they do with a click of a button!

This project uses python, js, css, html, sqlite, and pywebview to tie it all together.

Features:

Overall:

  • Your current file is remembered and opened immediately.
  • A little emoji that shows you're progress at a glance
  • The total profit/loss of the whole file at a glance
  • The win percentage of your total trades
  • Custom visibility settings where you can choose what you do/don't want to see
  • Can zoom in on photos and edit/delete trades easily right where they are
  • Responsive light/dark mode based off of your computer

Home page:

  • Quick, easy way to input trades (including automatic dates and pics).
  • A note section at the bottom where you can quickly input extra notes before you input it as a trade (just not pictures right now that won't work out too well)
  • Custom notifications.
  • Showing all from the day/week.
  • Color coding by day of week.

Stat Page:

  • Can quickly get stats based off of specific timeframes, variables, and even compare custom strategies in comparison to all of the data.

All Trades:

  • Can see, edit, and delete any inputs. Red outlines means details are missing.

Settings:

  • Set favorite settings, notifications, and choose what data you want to see.

New File:

  • Review settings before making a file.
