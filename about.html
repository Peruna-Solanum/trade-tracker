About:

This is Trade Tracker. You can input trade details, pics, etc. and will be able to automatically find the stats on your data without a subscription/doing things by hand, with more options to get data based off of custom strategies/groupings which to my knowledge doesn't really exist. Keep track of your gambling addiction today!

This project uses python, js, css, html, sqlite, and pywebview to tie it all together.

Features:

Overall

  • shows the connected file (that automatically open when you open the program)
  • a little emoji that shows you're progress at a glance
  • the total profit/loss of the whole file at a glance
  • the win percentage of your total trades
  • custom visibility settings where you can choose what you do/don't want to see
  • Can zoom in on photos and edit/delete trades easily right where they are
  • responsive light/dark mode based off of your computer

Home page:

  • An easy way to input trades (including automatic dates and pics if you wish)
  • A note section at the bottom where you can quickly input extra notes before you input it as a trade (just not pictures right now that won't work out too well)
  • custom notifications in the top, with choice of color
  • The option to show trades either for the day or for the week
  • Trades are colored based off of the day of the week for easy seeing

Stat Page:

  • Can quickly get stats based off of specific timeframes, variables, and even compare custom strategies in comparison to the rest of the trades

All Trades:

  • Can see/edit/delete all trades in the file, can also see if details are missing for some

Settings:

  • Can set favorite settings, notifications, and can hide/show certain variables

New File:

  • Can review settings before making a new file
